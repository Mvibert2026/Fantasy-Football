HOW TO READ THIS. Percentages are the chance a player is STILL ON THE BOARD
when your pick arrives. They come from simulating the other nine teams
drafting to consensus with noise. They do NOT depend on any projection of
how many points anyone scores, which is why they are the most reliable
numbers in this project.

Three columns because the room's discipline is unknown:
  TIGHT (sigma 5)  = everyone drafts close to consensus
  NORMAL (sigma 10) = about a round of slippage — the default assumption
  CHAOTIC (sigma 20) = reaches and slides everywhere
If a number is similar across all three, trust it. If it swings, the answer
depends on how your league behaves and you should plan for both.

==============================================================================
CAVEATS — read once now, not during the draft
==============================================================================
1. Opponents are modelled with a positional-need penalty derived from this
   league's roster rules (mechanical, ADR-034) and do NOT react to what you
   do. A real room responds to positional runs.
2. The noise level (sigma) is a guess. It is not fitted to any observed
   draft data, because none exists for this league. That is why every
   number is shown across three settings.
3. Opponent boards are drawn from a mixture of ranking sources -- currently
   one (FantasyPros ECR) -- so this is a no-op today, but it means a second
   source (MFL ADP, ADR-035) plugs in without changing this model's shape.
4. The board is consensus as of the date above and will move before Week 1.
   Re-run this script closer to the draft.
5. These are availability odds only. They say nothing about whether a
   player is GOOD — that question runs through a projection whose R-squared
   is 0.16-0.27, and is much less certain than anything on this sheet.
6. These are UNCONDITIONAL marginals (Prep mode): they average over every
   possible draft, not the one actually in progress. Mid-draft, a client
   simulator re-runs conditioned on real picks made -- see data-contract.md.
