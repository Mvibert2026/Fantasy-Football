1 · PACEINDICATOR — NEW 058 §A
The claim: how many at this position have gone, against how many would be expected to have gone by this pick. A bare signed integer cannot make that claim — +2 does not say whether two more were taken or two fewer remain, and ±0 currently means both "measured on pace" and "no baseline exists". Those are different claims and the null vocabulary already forbids collapsing them.

THE RULE
Pace renders as a phrase, never a bare sign. A measured zero renders on pace. No consensus baseline renders —. A baseline that exists but cannot yet be compared renders not yet and names the missing input. Nothing renders ±0. DEF has no published replacement level, so DEF pace is — until one exists — adding the row does not mean inventing the number.
