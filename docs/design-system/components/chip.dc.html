THE RULE THAT CARRIES CREDIBILITY
A pill must never be sortable and must never act as a filter. Archetype labels are descriptive and do not influence a rank — the moment a pill becomes clickable-as-filter, it starts to look like an input to the model, and the product's central claim is that it is not.
