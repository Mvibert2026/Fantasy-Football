Ranked priorities — filterable

Ordering logic: urgency × irreversibility × unblock-count, not raw impact. Items that become impossible if delayed (ADP snapshots, per-pick logging, mock volume before the draft) outrank larger-value items with no clock on them.

Blocked on a decision you can make today

  • FantasyPros paid tier (~$72/yr). Free tier hard-caps at 10 rows/response with no working pagination — 40 players max, all already inside the 145 covered by the rank-to-points curve. It does not reach the 233 players lacking projections. Buy or formally accept partial coverage; it can't be engineered around.
  • Per-pick mock state schema shape. Drafted-counts-per-team vs. raw ordered pick list. ADR-043's ALTER TABLE ADD COLUMN pattern already exists to do it non-destructively.
  • Per-position rank correlation aggregation. Position-weighted single number vs. reported separately. Pure design call, zero data dependency, blocks a guardrail gap.
All three are cheap. All three have been sitting. This is the highest-yield hour you could spend on the project.
