## Request
Making the playoff outranks playoff-week schedule quality

Founder's own words:

> "cant win the playoffs if you don't make them"

## Why it matters / PM's read

Founder's ruling on the caveat PM attached to FR-105's playoff-weeks proposal.

**Recorded as a priority ordering, not a rejection.** A weeks-16/17 schedule edge is worth nothing to
a team that finishes 5th. So any playoff-weeks analysis must be framed as a **tiebreaker among
otherwise-equivalent players**, never as a reason to prefer a weaker player who happens to have a
good week-17 matchup.

This mirrors the sleeper/bust asymmetry (FR-094, FR-096): a weak signal is admissible as a
tiebreaker and inadmissible as a reason to move someone materially. It also aligns with `CLAUDE.md`
§7's existing note that a slow start is unusually costly in this league.

**Consequence for sequencing:** anything that improves the odds of *making* the 4-team playoff
outranks anything that improves performance once there. Whoever runs FR-105 must state this
constraint in the write-up rather than presenting a playoff-weeks edge in isolation.
