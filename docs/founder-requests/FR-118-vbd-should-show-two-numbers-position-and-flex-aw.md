## Request
VBD should show two numbers — positional value and flex/alternatives-aware value

Founder's own words:

> "VBD probably should show me both numbers value of that position, and based on flex and other options (I guess score tells me vs other options in the area and available on the board."

## Why it matters / PM's read

Two different questions a drafter asks, currently answered by one number:

1. **"How good is this player at his position?"** — value over positional replacement. What VBD
   already shows.
2. **"How good is this player against what I could otherwise do with this roster spot?"** — value
   against the best flex-eligible alternative actually available on the board right now.

The second is the decision-relevant one and it does not exist today. It is also the fix for the
TE over-suggestion he reported separately: TE replacement sits at TE10 while a tight end has won a
flex slot in **2 of 26 seasons tested**, so positional VBD measures a tight end against the wrong
alternative.

**Show both, labelled.** Not a blend — this project's rule is that ranking sources stay separate and
never silently converge, and the same logic applies to two value definitions. A single merged number
would hide exactly the disagreement the founder wants to see.
