## Request
Every strategy arm needs roster awareness — wide constraints, but real ones

Founder's own words:

> "all of these likely need some awareness of roster at some point, it can be wide constraints but cant pick 3 Tight Ends because that's how the draft happens to fall to you (maybe unlikely but you get the point)"

## Why it matters / PM's read

**This is a real defect in the current simulation arms, not a refinement.**

The pre-commitment defines VBD as *"take the highest-VBD available player, subject only to §5.1
legality. No positional rules whatsoever."* Legality only forces the roster to be *fillable*. Nothing
stops an arm from taking a third tight end because the board happened to fall that way — and once
taken, that player occupies a bench slot delivering near-zero marginal value, since only one TE ever
starts.

The founder's framing is the right one: **wide constraints, not tight ones.** Not positional quotas,
which would smuggle strategy into the baseline; a cap on absurdity. A useful form is diminishing
marginal value — the second TE is worth much less than the first, the third essentially nothing —
which is arithmetic about the roster, not a strategy opinion.

**This may partly explain the FR-085 results.** If arms are taking positionally redundant players,
some of the null margins are being generated by roster inefficiency common to all arms rather than by
the strategies themselves.
