## Request

Founder's own words, 2026-07-30, relayed verbatim in the PM's brief for FR-136 Q1:

> "Hopefully we have lots of components in our rankings, we want it to be proprietary (youd think
> we'd have projections then too)."

Alongside his standing bar, also verbatim:

> "If I don't have those three things in place, I don't want to use the tool for my real draft."

And his data precondition, same day:

> "don't run a test on bad data, don't run recommendations on the wrong ppr"

## Why it matters

It is a **three-part** expectation, and the parenthesis is the load-bearing part. He is not asking
for components *or* projections — he is saying that if the ranking is genuinely built from
components, projections fall out of it for free. That is correct as engineering, and it is the exact
gap: `experiments/bottomup/components/` does produce real per-player component projections that beat
naive persistence at all four positions, and the shipped board's `projected_points` is instead a log
curve fitted to consensus rank (R² 0.16–0.27). Two different objects, and the one he is describing
is not the one on screen.

"Proprietary" is the same sentence's other half and it is measured: the shipped board correlates
with consensus at **ρ = 0.972 across the top 100**, and its entire independent content is a QB/TE
positional tilt generated by four slopes and four replacement levels.
