## Headline findings

1. **The mandate's own description of the workflow is stale in ways that change the answers.**
   Worktree isolation already exists — three agent worktrees live under `.claude/worktrees/`, and
   one branch (`a246696`, the thread-018 FantasyPros backfill) is **finished, unmerged, and
   invisible to every index**: silent non-delivery, the quieter cousin of silent overwrite. And ID
   collisions are endemic, not a one-off — at least four in ~24 hours across two namespaces
   (threads 043, 049, 053; ADR-048), while the announced fix (PM allocates ≥100) is structurally
   broken because `tools/handoffs.py` allocates `max(all IDs)+1`, so the first PM thread at 100
   pushes agents into the PM's range. Fix: slug-authored threads, IDs assigned at sync, PM writes
   through an outbox directory it cannot force-write from (W1); the binding ceiling after that is
   PM sequencing plus the mandatory shared write-back docs, addressed by W3/W4.

2. **The consensus input to the entire product is the wrong scoring format, and the product is
   consensus.** The ECR source is a standard-scoring mirror with no half-PPR variant while the
   league is half-PPR (`src/ingest_rankings.py:25-36`) — known since thread 018, with the fix
   sitting in that same unmerged worktree branch, and no executable check pinning the stored
   format (T1). Separately, the board is *openly* 100% consensus-anchored by design
   (`make_board.py:36-38`: same rank → same projection; player-level edge structurally zero) —
   it is the baseline, and the baseline is currently also the product. Consequence for the
   roadmap claim: the "beat consensus by ~2028/2029" clock **has not started**, because the
   independent board the comparison needs does not exist yet; both dates are also wrong on their
   own terms (~2029 is a moot 14-test-family figure whose parameter D-001 deleted; the repo's own
   ~2028 resolution exists in `status.md:1785` and was lost to a revert). And "unclaimable" is
   not "undecidable": a pre-registered descriptive adoption rule — the device ADR-E §9 already
   uses — is runnable the day an independent board exists (S2).

3. **The correctness floor and the guardrail machinery both have coverage gaps exactly where a
   founder would hit them first.** Suspensions, NFL roster status (retired/IR/practice squad),
   and snapshot freshness are entirely unbuilt; positive bye coverage is untested (fail-open);
   5 of 20 table stakes are not built at all (T4–T6 are the expensive ones, T5 the cheapest big
   win). On the statistics side, the holdout seal binds against accident only: the ADR-C
   registration gate has **zero production callsites** while an unrelated same-named function
   (`ds.load_season`) makes reads look gated, the unlock is self-service, and no test audits the
   access logs — cheap fixes (H1–H4), and they become urgent the moment ADR-E's first
   confirmatory run approaches. One clean bill worth stating: `UNSEAL_LOG.md` does not exist —
   the 2025 holdout has never been unsealed — and PR-002's null was reported and built upon,
   the best evidence the discipline is real.
