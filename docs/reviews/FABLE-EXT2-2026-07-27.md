### V3 — vacated/arrived opportunity features (work order R1)

**Franchise canonicalisation** (measured this session): the DB uses modern codes from 2003
onward; map `{OAK→LV, SD→LAC, STL→LA, JAC→JAX}` applies only to 1999–2002 rows, i.e. only to
long-arm folds whose feature season is ≤ 2002.

**Current-franchise assignment** for player p, target season t: modal canonical team over p's
REG appearances in **weeks 1–4 of season t**, reading only (player_id, team, week) — never a
production quantity. If p has no weeks-1–4 appearance: current := his t−1 franchise,
`changed_team` := 0, and p counts as NOT retained in others' vacated shares.

**Registered look-ahead disclosure:** weeks-1–4 roster membership is target-season
information. It is the approximation the R1 spec blesses ("week-1 roster… mild look-ahead"),
widened to weeks 1–4 to avoid misclassifying short absences as departures. It can advantage
the model over B1 precisely where the market also knew (August injuries, retirements,
holdouts). Every report of V3 carries this flag.

**Registered blind spot:** arrivals are visible only via t−1 production, so **rookies are
invisible to arrival shares** — a team drafting a first-round RB shows zero arrived
competition. This mutes the measurable situation effect at RB and QB; a null result does not
eliminate rookie-driven situation change as a channel.

**Six features appended to the shared S1 vector** (per-position ridges weight them freely):
1. `changed_team` (0/1)
2. `vac_rec_share` — share of current franchise's t−1 receiving volume (targets in the usage
   arm, receptions in the long arm — same availability rule as `rec_vol`) produced by players
   not retained by that franchise in t
3. `vac_carry_share` — same for carries
4. `vac_att_share` — same for pass attempts
5. `arr_rec_share` — t−1 receiving volume produced elsewhere by arrivals at the current
   franchise (excluding p himself), over the franchise's t−1 receiving volume
6. `arr_carry_share` — same for carries

Zero denominators (expansion HOU 2002) → share 0. Both arms run, same folds as V1
(long 2002–2024, usage 2012–2024). One configuration; no tuning passes.

**Registered predictions (the point of this section):**
- Direction: improvement concentrates at **RB**; smaller at **QB**; **WR/TE ≈ unchanged**
  (|Δ| ≤ 0.02 on Δτ_b vs B1). This is the session-2 consensus-gap fingerprint read forward.
- Magnitude, RB: Δτ_b vs B1 from +0.041 to **+0.05..+0.08**; consensus gap (2021–2024
  descriptive, currently −0.110) narrows by **one-third to one-half**.
- Magnitude, QB: gap vs B1 (−0.108) shrinks toward **−0.06..−0.09** but stays negative;
  consensus gap (−0.223) narrows by ≤ one-quarter. Low confidence — QB situation is
  scheme/coaching more than vacated volume, and the rookie blind spot bites here too.
- Co-primary (VBD-capture): same directional pattern; RB positive.
- **Falsified if:** RB improves on NEITHER co-primary vs V1 AND the RB consensus gap does not
  narrow. Then the vacated-opportunity form of the situation-information diagnosis is refuted
  and is reported as such — a valuable elimination, not a disappointment. A material WR jump
  (> +0.03) alongside a flat RB would *contradict* the fingerprint reading, not rescue it.
- Ceiling: session-2 Q4 bands govern unchanged; any season-points R² > 0.40 triggers the §8
  audit posture.
- Decision rule: V3 replaces V1 as the carried candidate only if RB improves on the
  registered co-primaries without degrading WR/TE beyond −0.02 Δτ_b.
