skill-position codes present 2002–2024 are QB/RB/WR/TE (+42 FB picks, excluded below).

### The mechanism, stated exactly

Rookies are excluded from the v0 universe by registration (prior-season finish defines it),
so **vacated opportunity flowing TO a rookie is invisible to every variant so far**: V3/V5's
arrival shares are built from t−1 production, which rookies do not have. A team that loses
180 carries and drafts a first-round RB shows high vacated share and zero arrived
competition — the model credits the remaining veterans with an open backfield that is
actually spoken for. ECR prices the rookie; B1 and our model do not. This was the registered
blind spot of the V3/V5 family and is the leading remaining hypothesis for the RB consensus
gap (−0.110, unmoved by V5). Draft capital is the natural carrier: it is public in April,
production-independent, and fully pre-season (no look-ahead).

### V7 — V5 + rookie-arrival draft capital (one configuration)

Base: **V5 exactly** (self-excluded vacated shares, composition pipeline at all four
positions, both arms, same folds: long 2002–2024, usage 2012–2024). Three features appended
to the shared S1 vector (per-position ridges weight them freely):

1. `rook_cap_same` — Σ over rookies drafted in the season-t draft by p's current franchise
   at p's position: `1/sqrt(overall_pick)`. Position match is exact code equality on
   {QB, RB, WR, TE}; FB is excluded (declared: 42 picks over 23 drafts, not modern workload
   competition).
2. `rook_top64_same` — indicator that any such pick is ≤ 64 overall (rounds 1–2).
3. `rook_cap_x_vac` — `rook_cap_same ×` the position-relevant **self-excluded** vacated
   share from V5: `vac_att_share` for QB, `vac_carry_share` for RB, `vac_rec_share` for
   WR/TE. This is the mechanism term: vacated volume in the presence of high rookie capital
   flows to the rookie, not to returning veterans.

**Current-franchise assignment:** identical to V5 (weeks-1–4 modal canonical team), carrying
V3/V5's registered look-ahead disclosure unchanged. No new look-ahead enters: the season-t
draft is complete before ADP forms. A universe player can never be a season-t draftee
(universe requires a t−1 finish), so the V5 self-exclusion issue cannot arise here.

**Team-code crosswalk (required, structural):** `draft_picks` uses PFR-era codes; the
experiment uses modern canonical codes. Declared mapping: GNB→GB, KAN→KC, NOR→NO, NWE→NE,
SFO→SF, TAM→TB, SDG→LAC, STL→LA, OAK→LV, LVR→LV, LAR→LA; identity otherwise (JAX stays JAX).
A test must assert every crosswalked `draft_picks` team code for seasons ≥ 2003 appears in
the canonical team set observed in `player_weekly_stats`; a code that does not resolve is a
build failure, not a silent zero (the T3/T9 lesson).

### Registered predictions (direction, magnitude, positions — the point of this section)

- **Direction:** improvement concentrates at **RB** — rookie RB usurpation is the most
  common and most immediate same-position competition (first-round RBs take over backfields
  in year 1). WR smaller and positive (rookie WRs ramp slower; target competition is
  diffuse). TE ≈ unchanged (rookie TEs are rarely productive in year 1).
- **Magnitude, RB (usage arm, vs V5's numbers):** Δτ_b vs B1 improves from +0.057 by
  **+0.010 to +0.030**; consensus gap (2021–2024 descriptive, −0.110 at V5) narrows by
  **0.02 to 0.05** — a fifth to a half. Co-primary (VBD-capture) positive, same pattern.
- **Magnitude, WR/TE:** WR |Δτ_b| ≤ 0.02 (positive side); TE |Δτ_b| ≤ 0.01.
- **QB: descriptive only, no prediction stakes.** QB modelling is closed (six
  configurations, two sessions); V7's QB numbers are reported for completeness and do not
  reopen it. A rookie-QB effect on incumbents is real but is an availability channel, and
  availability channels at QB are exactly where V3 leaked.
- **Falsified if:** RB fails to improve on BOTH co-primaries vs V5 AND the RB consensus gap
  does not narrow by ≥ 0.02. Then rookie-driven situation change **as expressible via
  same-position draft capital** is eliminated as the explanation of the RB gap, and is
  reported as an elimination. Contradiction guard (unchanged from V3): a material WR jump
  (> +0.03) alongside a flat RB contradicts the mechanism reading, not rescues it.
- **What remains if falsified, stated now:** (a) rookie *inclusion* — rookies themselves
  mispriced, the C3 universe arm, still reserved for the return-week registration;
  (b) ECR's non-box-score information (camp/beat reports, holdout and injury pricing —
  partially the T-series' job, not a model feature); (c) coaching/scheme change (R2, gated
  on data); (d) no single channel — ECR as an aggregate of many small edges, in which case
  no feature family closes the gap and the labelled-overlay product posture (D7) is the
  correct end state, not a way station.
- **Ceiling:** session-2 Q4 bands govern unchanged; any season-points R² > 0.40 triggers
  the ADR-E §8 audit posture before any number is reported.

**Decision rule:** V7 replaces V5 as the carried candidate only if RB improves on ≥ 1
registered co-primary vs V5 without degrading WR/TE beyond −0.02 Δτ_b and with no audit
flag outstanding. The consensus-gap movement is the mechanism verdict (descriptive, n=4,
no p-value — per standing language rules), not the carry criterion.
