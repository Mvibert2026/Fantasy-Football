## The prototype results, in full (per the mandate's reporting requirements)

Protocol: registration frozen in the P1 review before fitting; expanding-window walk-forward
(train ≤ t−1, predict t); universe = prior-season positional finish within QB20/RB45/WR60/TE20,
rookies excluded (v0, registered); actuals scored by the real league engine; 2025 never read.
**Variant count: 2 model configurations** (V1 registered; V2 = QB TD-cap 0.6, run, rejected)
plus one defect fix (F1, availability-boundary denominator — the exact ADR-E §4.3 hazard,
found via a fold-2004 R² blowup). With metric/universe/baselines frozen pre-fit and 8
position-arm cells examined, the WR result (consistent across both arms, CI clear of zero) is
the credible headline; single-cell 95% intervals should be read with the 8-cell denominator
in mind. Full per-season JSON: `experiments/bottomup/results/`.
