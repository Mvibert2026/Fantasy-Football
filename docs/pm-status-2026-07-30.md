## 2. What the research said — the short version

**The headline has not changed: we do not beat consensus ADP at any position.** RB is the one
position where the test has power, and we lose there. Nothing in our rankings currently justifies
departing from consensus, which is why the app still does not show them.

**Six things closed today**, all recorded in `docs/strategic-insights.md`:

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Zero RB?** | **No.** P(title) +0.001 [−0.020, +0.023]. The reason matters more than the number: **plain VBD already takes its first RB in round 6.3**, so the test compared 6.3 vs 10.7. VBD is already most of the way to Zero RB without being told. |
| **Your "tails" hypothesis** (skew and kurtosis) | **Decisive null**, and it fails *upstream* — a player's shape does not persist year to year (6 of 6 null). The shrinkage estimator drove τ² to **exactly zero**: given every chance, it concluded there is no real between-player variation in shape at all. Even an oracle arm using the target season's own shape makes the error *worse*. |
| **Which positions are volatile?** | Per player WR is worst. **Per roster slot the order inverts: TE is the most volatile thing on your roster** (1.002 vs WR 0.545) — one skill slot with no diversification, while three WR starters plus flex average out. Your slot intuition was right. |
| **Does the RB dead zone still exist?** | **Cannot honestly be called moved or vanished.** RB13–24 is null and points the *wrong* way. What changed is the far end: **RB37+ improved by +48.3 [+21.6, +75.1] against late WR.** So "you can wait on RB" is supported — via the *late* tier improving, not the middle recovering. Different behaviour implied. |
| **Consensus or ADP?** | Indistinguishable pooled (54.6%, CI crosses 50%), and one season carries it. **The keeper: early-round disagreements favour the experts 59.5% [0.506, 0.678], effect +15.5 pts vs +3.1 late.** When the room and the experts disagree in the first five rounds, lean expert. After that it does not matter. |
| **Late-round sleepers?** | **No flag ships** — nothing separated hits from misses. But the base rate is a real finding: **~1 in 4 late picks returns startable value**, holding on the holdout. Late rounds are a volume game, not a selection game. |
