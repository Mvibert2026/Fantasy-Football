## Ask

Four measured facts about `data/nfl.db`, found while running factor batch 7. **Three are
corrections to what the project believes; one is a request.** All measured this session, none
estimated.

### 1. `pbp` has NO `yards_after_catch` column, and it starts in 2009 — not 1999

`docs/research/analyst-factor-sweep-2026-07-30.md` §2c row N13/N16 and `CLAUDE.md` §5 both describe
play-by-play as "back to 1999". **In `nfl.db` the `pbp` table has 24 columns**, and the full list is:

```
season week posteam defteam play_id game_id down yardline_100 goal_to_go pass rush
pass_attempt rush_attempt complete_pass touchdown yards_gained air_yards
rusher_player_id receiver_player_id passer_player_id xpass wp
half_seconds_remaining score_differential ingested_at
```

`min(season) = 2009`, 816,856 rows. **No `yards_after_catch`, no `epa`, no `yards_after_contact`.**

**No action requested on the table itself** — batch 7 routed around it. What I am asking for is that
whoever owns the ingest confirms whether the 2009 floor and the column subset are *deliberate*
(a projection chosen at ingest time) or *incidental*. It changes what future factor batches can
plan against, and "pbp is 1999+" is currently stated as fact in two places.

### 2. `player_weekly_stats.receiving_yards_after_catch` is identically ZERO for 2000–2005

Measured, RB rows, `season_type = 'REG'`:

| season | 1999 | 2000–2005 | 2006 | 2007 | … | 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Σ `receiving_yards_after_catch` | 378 | **0 in every year** | 16,237 | 16,051 | | 19,043 |
| Σ `receptions` | 1,957 | 2,000–2,400/yr | 2,217 | 2,228 | | 2,301 |

The rows exist (1,300–1,500 non-null per season throughout) — **the values are zero**, so a `NOT
NULL` check does not catch it and any mean computed over 1999–2005 is silently wrong by 100%.

**Requested:** if `docs/data-availability.md` has a per-column coverage table, add
`receiving_yards_after_catch` with a **2006** floor. If it does not, tell me and I will raise it as
its own request rather than assume.

### 3. `snap_counts` is keyed on PFR ids and the crosswalk is not in any documented rebuild path

`snap_counts` (324,611 rows, 2013–2025) has `pfr_player_id` and **no gsis id**. Everything else in
the modelling path is gsis. Batch 7 joined it through `player_ids` (pivot on `mfl_id`, sources
`gsis` and `pfr`, 7,546 usable pairs) and measured the result:

| | |
|---|---|
| RB player-seasons matched to a gsis id | **99.34%** |
| the same, weighted by offensive snaps | **99.55%** |

**Requested:** confirm `player_ids` is regenerated by `scripts/rebuild_database.py`, or say plainly
that it is one of the artifacts `docs/can-we-rebuild-the-database.md` lists as committed-because-it-
cannot-be. Any model that uses snap counts inherits the crosswalk's fate and nothing currently says
what that is.

### 4. A request: is `participation` complete, and is it still being published?

Batch 7 built red-zone snap rates from `participation`.`offense_players` × `pbp`.`yardline_100`.
Measured coverage:

| season | 2016 | 2017 | 2018 | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rows | 48,434 | 47,998 | 47,875 | 48,034 | 48,513 | 50,714 | 50,150 | 46,168 | 45,919 | 45,184 |
| games | 267 | 267 | 267 | 267 | 269 | 285 | 284 | 285 | 285 | 285 |
| empty `offense_players` | 4,093 | 4,048 | 4,153 | 4,058 | 4,152 | 4,388 | 4,292 | **0** | **0** | **0** |

The empties are **entirely non-scrimmage plays** — restricted to `rush_attempt = 1 OR
pass_attempt = 1`, missingness is **0.0000** in every season, including 2016. So the table is usable
as-is.

**But the 2023 discontinuity is unexplained** (empties drop to exactly zero and row counts drop by
~4,000) and I do not know whether that is an upstream format change or an ingest change here.
**Requested:** one sentence on what changed at 2023, and whether nflverse still publishes
participation — my understanding is that it was discontinued at some point, and if these are the
last seasons we will ever get, that bounds every future factor built on it.

## Why

Batch 7 tested six factors and **none was blocked** — that is the good news and it is worth stating.
But two of the four facts above are places where a future batch would compute a confidently wrong
number without any error being raised: a YAC mean over 2000–2005 (silently zero) and a snap-count
join that fails silently if the crosswalk is not rebuilt. Neither is expensive to pin down now and
both are expensive to discover inside a result.

## Done looks like

A reply on this thread with: (1) deliberate-or-incidental on the `pbp` projection; (2) the 2006 YAC
floor recorded wherever per-column coverage lives, or a statement that no such place exists;
(3) a yes/no on `player_ids` being rebuildable; (4) one sentence on the 2023 participation
discontinuity and whether the source is still live.
