## The three decisions that are expensive to change later

1. **The position-colour rule for the grid** (`DRAFT-MIDDLE-PANE.md` §1.1). Position hue tints and
   owns the left edge; the semantic accents are banned from the grid; depletion is the fast channel.
   This is in every cell, so it is the one thing here worth deciding before a line is written.
2. **The middle pane is one tab set, not two.** The grid leaves the pane and becomes a full-width
   view. Four of the five requests belong in that rectangle; one does not.
3. **Supplied values are marked by shape and words, not hue** (`SUPPLIED-VALUES.md`). Both controls
   currently use `--acc`, which already means "good" on the board.
