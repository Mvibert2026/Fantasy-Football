# Design brief — 2026-07-31

**Five items. All five are blocked on design; none can be built without a spec.**

Send this whole file. Screenshots are in `frontend/e2e/artifacts/` (filenames named per item below) —
they are the current app, captured within the last 24 hours, not mock-ups.

---

## Standing rules — these have not changed

1. **The built app is the reference. Design catches up to code, not the reverse.** Where a spec and
   the shipped app disagree, the app is right unless the spec explicitly overrides it and says why.
2. **Phone is priority 4, and currently paused** at the founder's instruction. Design desktop first.
   Do not spend effort on mobile layouts in this round.
3. **Absent beats wrong.** This app never fabricates a value. Where data is missing it says so, in
   place, with a reason. Any design that hides an absence behind a plausible-looking default will be
   rejected — that rule has already killed three proposals.
4. **A control that cannot act is not a control** (`INERT-CONTROLS.md`). Render the fact, not a dead
   affordance.
5. Existing token vocabulary lives in `SUPPLIED-VALUES.md` — including the established marker for
   "you put this here" (dotted underline + lowercase monospace label, never the accent colour).
   Reuse it rather than inventing new markers.

## 1. Light theme is too bright — HIGHEST PRIORITY

Founder's words: *"Light fiew maybe needs a touch up, it's very bright, it could use some shading."*

This is the only item he has raised unprompted about visual comfort, and it affects every screen.

**What is needed:** a shading strategy for light mode — surface elevation, panel separation, and where
grey should sit against white. Right now the light theme reads as flat white with hairline borders.

**Constraint:** dark mode is the mode he mostly uses and it is not broken. Do not restyle dark mode to
match a new light system; the two need to stay independently coherent.

**Screenshots:** any `fr067-*`, `fr082-*` — all captured in the current theme.

## 2. The draft rankings pane reads dated

Founder's words: *"there are parts that feel old… the rankings in the left on draft may need to be a
wider pane."*

Two things bundled and they should be separated in your response:

- **Look and feel.** What specifically reads as dated, and what replaces it. Be concrete — this is the
  screen he spends the whole draft looking at.
- **Width.** He suspects the left rankings pane is too narrow. Related to item 3.

**Screenshots:** `fr067-fr087-draft-board-1180w.png` and `fr067-fr087-draft-board-1500w.png` — the
same screen at two widths, so you can see how it currently adapts.

**Note:** column alignment on that board was fixed yesterday with one shared column definition. Do not
propose a layout that requires header and rows to be defined separately again.

## 3. Resizable / pop-out panes

Founder's words: *"from a width standpoint, it would be interesting to allow me to drag to change the
pane size as needed… These are ideas, not suggestions, design should weigh in based on best
practices."*

**He has explicitly asked you to push back if dragging is the wrong answer.** Options worth weighing:
drag handles, preset layout modes, a pop-out window for one pane, or simply better default widths
that make resizing unnecessary.

Context that should inform this: during a live draft he is under a clock, on a laptop, with a real
draft board open elsewhere. Anything requiring fiddly mouse precision mid-draft is a bad answer.

## 4. Player profile

Founder's words: *"The player profile work still needs to occur."*

The player card exists and has been extended repeatedly, but it has never had a designed information
hierarchy — it has accreted. What belongs at the top, what is secondary, what should be behind
disclosure.

**One new input that changes this:** archetype is being surfaced on the card now (see item 5). Design
the profile knowing that a short descriptive label is arriving near the top.

**Screenshots:** `fr083-player-card-westwood-adp-block.png`,
`fr083-player-card-standard-league-adp-block.png`, `fr079-player-card-westwood-history.png` — the
same card across two league formats.

## 5. Archetype placement — spec the target, not the interim

Founder's words: *"We need to get archetype built and I'd like to see it towards the top of the card,
or in prep there is space next to the names to the right before position comes into play."*

An interim version is shipping now: the card previously claimed archetype did not exist, which was
false, and that is being corrected.

**What design owns is the target treatment**, and it has to survive an honesty problem:

- `RB_COMMITTEE` currently covers **62.7%** of running backs
- `TE_SECONDARY_RECEIVER` covers **51.0%** of tight ends
- Roughly **34% of eligible players match no archetype at all**

So the label is frequently uninformative and often absent. **A confident-looking chip would overstate
what this data supports.** The taxonomy is being revised (`docs/ranking/archetypes-proposal.md`
proposes scored dimensions with a derived chip rather than a single label, plus a hard rule that no
label may exceed 35% of its position and unclassified may not exceed 10%).

**Design for the revised system**, and say how a low-confidence or absent archetype should read
differently from a strong one.
