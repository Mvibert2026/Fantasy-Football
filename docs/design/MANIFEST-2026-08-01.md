## The decision you asked for

**Status wins the identity strip and the archetype chip leaves it entirely — but not on frequency.**
On consequence: a status can invalidate the pick, an archetype interprets it. One is a gate, the other
is commentary, and only the gate belongs on the line he cannot avoid reading.

Frequency decides one thing: **how loud status is allowed to be.** Present on 100% of players means the
common state must cost almost nothing — so healthy is a word and a hollow dot, no box and no colour,
and the box and hue arrive with the consequence.

## And one placement that dissolves a bad tradeoff

**Status does not enter the column drop order, because it is not a column.** It rides the name cell it
qualifies — zero width, undroppable, attached to the identity it describes. A column would have
competed with the name at exactly the widths where width is scarce.

## Three things worth flagging

1. **`suspension_flag` is `False` for all 510 players.** A uniform `False` across every row is the
   signature of an unpopulated field, not a league with no suspensions. Any "healthy" derived from it
   would be fabrication.
2. **The news feed needs six empty states, not two**, and four of them are unrenderable without two
   contract fields — a fetch outcome and a declared coverage list. Design cannot fix that afterwards.
3. **Global zero is the dangerous one.** A league-wide feed reporting nothing is more likely broken
   than quiet, and draft morning is exactly when he would trust it and be wrong to.
