## The headline

**The grid is unblocked. No position hue changes.** Engineering can pick up FR-044.

But the reason is not the one I gave in round one, and the correction matters: **there is no category
convention.** FantasyPros badges WR green and RB blue; Yahoo badges WR orange and RB green. The two
leaders disagree about the same five positions, so "follow the convention" cannot be executed as
written — and the founder prefers the product whose palette is further from ours. With no convention
to match, the only binding constraints are internal, our hues already satisfy them, and changing
them would cost a retrofit across every screen for nothing.

## Three things carried from the images

1. **A fourth cause for the inert-control rule — gated.** Their padlocked controls are correct
   because the action exists and is purchasable. None of our six is, so round one's list stands —
   but the rule now states the principle instead of asserting the outcome.
2. **Their jump-nav is dressed as tabs.** A filled pill implies the other sections are hidden; they
   are not. If we adopt the pattern on the player sheet it renders as links, not as a selected state.
3. **Their meters have a valence bug.** All three fill green, so high bust risk would read as
   excellent. Our season dots colour each segment from that season's own sign.
